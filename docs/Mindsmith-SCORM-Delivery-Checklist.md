# Mindsmith SCORM Delivery — One-Page Checklist

**For:** Content owners / VECTRA ops  
**Goal:** Progress %, score, and completion in Otto Group Academy LMS

---

## Do this

1. Author the course in **Mindsmith**.
2. Export / Download as **SCORM 1.2** (ZIP file).
3. In LMS: **Admin → Courses → Upload SCORM ZIP**.
4. Assign the course to learners.
5. Track status and completion in **Admin → Reports** (CSV export available).

---

## Do not use

- Mindsmith **published URL**  
  → Not supported in this LMS. Progress and completion will not be tracked.

---

## What reports will show (SCORM)

| State | Progress |
|-------|----------|
| Not opened | 0% |
| Started | ~10% |
| Completed / passed | 100% |

Score is recorded when the SCORM package reports it.

---

## One-line summary for stakeholders

> *Mindsmith is for authoring; SCORM 1.2 export is required for LMS progress and completion tracking.*

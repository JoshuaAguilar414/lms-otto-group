# Otto Group Academy LMS — Learner Guide

**Audience:** Facility and Business Partner learners  
**Product:** Otto Group Academy Learning Management System  
**Live site:** [https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com](https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com)

This guide explains how to register (or receive an invitation), activate your account, sign in, and access your assigned training courses.

---

## Before you start — what you need

| Item | Where to get it |
|------|-----------------|
| **Corporate email** | Your work email address |
| **Company ID** | From the VECTRA participant / invitation list |
| **Stakeholder group** | **Facility** or **Business Partner** (as listed for your organization) |
| **LMS website URL** | [https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com](https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com) |

Registration only works if your **Company ID** and **stakeholder group** are on the approved VECTRA participant roster.

---

## Two ways to get an account

You can join in one of two ways:

| Path | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Path A — Self-registration** | You sign up yourself at the Register page |
| **Path B — Invitation email** | You receive an email inviting you to activate your account |

Both paths end the same way: you receive an **activation email**, set a password, then sign in.

---

## Path A — Self-registration

### Step 1 — Open the registration page

1. Go to [https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com](https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com).
2. Click **Register here** (from the sign-in page), or open:  
   [https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com/register](https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com/register)

### Step 2 — Fill in the form

Enter:

1. **Email address** — your corporate email
2. **Full name** — first and last name
3. **Company ID** — from the participant / invitation list
4. **Stakeholder group** — choose one:
   - **Business Partner**, or
   - **Facility**
5. **Organizational name** — fills in automatically when your Company ID matches the roster
   - If several organizations share the same ID, pick yours from the dropdown

### Step 3 — Submit registration

1. Click **Register**.
2. If successful, you will see a confirmation message.
3. Check your email for: **“Activate your Otto Group training account”**.

**If registration fails:** Your Company ID may not be on the roster, or the email is already registered. Contact your VECTRA training contact for help.

---

## Path B — Invitation email

You may receive an invitation email before you register yourself.

### Step 1 — Wait for the invitation email

You will receive an email with subject:

> **Activate your Otto Group training account**

It contains an **Activate your account** link.

- The link is valid for **7 days**.
- If it expires, contact your VECTRA training contact to request a new invitation.

### Step 2 — No self-registration needed

You do **not** need to visit the Register page if you received an invitation. Go straight to the activation link in the email.

---

## Activate your account (both paths)

### Step 1 — Open the activation link

Click the link in your email. It opens:

`https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com/activate?token=...`

### Step 2 — Create your password

Enter and confirm a password that meets these rules:

- At least **10 characters**
- At least one **uppercase** letter
- At least one **lowercase** letter
- At least one **number**

### Step 3 — Activate

1. Click **Activate account**.
2. You will be redirected to the **Sign in** page automatically.

---

## Sign in

### Step 1 — Open the login page

Go to: [https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com/login](https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com/login)

### Step 2 — Enter credentials

- **Corporate email**
- **Password** (the one you set during activation)

### Step 3 — Click **Sign in**

You will land on your **Overview** dashboard.

---

## Access your courses

Training courses are assigned to you. They will appear on your dashboard once they have been assigned to your account.

### Step 1 — Go to your dashboard

After sign-in you will see:

- **Overview** — summary of assigned, in-progress, and completed courses
- **My courses** — list of all assigned training

Open **My courses** from the menu, or go to:  
[https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com/dashboard/courses](https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com/dashboard/courses)

### Step 2 — Check course status

Each course shows one of these statuses:

| Status | Meaning | Button |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Not Started** | You have not opened it yet | **Start course** |
| **In Progress** | You started but did not finish | **Continue course** |
| **Completed** | You finished the training | **Review course** |

### Step 3 — Launch the course

1. Click **Start course**, **Continue course**, or **Review course**.
2. The training player opens in your browser.
3. Complete the modules as directed in the course.

### Step 4 — Progress is saved automatically

- Your place is saved when you leave and return.
- Use **Continue course** to resume from your last position.
- Your progress, score, and completion status are saved as you work through the course.

**If you see “No courses assigned”:** No training has been assigned to your account yet. Contact your VECTRA training contact if you were expecting a course.

---

## Forgot password

1. On the sign-in page ([https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com/login](https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com/login)), click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your corporate email.
3. Click **Send reset link**.
4. Check your email for: **“Reset your Otto Group Academy password”**
5. Open the link (valid for **2 hours**).
6. Set a new password and sign in again.

        ↓

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| “No organization found for this Company ID” | Confirm your Company ID and stakeholder group with your VECTRA training contact |
| No activation email | Check spam/junk; wait a few minutes and try again |
| Activation link expired (7 days) | Contact your VECTRA training contact to request a new invitation |
| Cannot sign in | Use **Forgot password**; confirm you activated your account first |
| No courses on dashboard | Contact your VECTRA training contact — training may not be assigned yet |
| Course will not load | Try another browser; use desktop or mobile Chrome, Edge, or Safari |

## Summary

You need a **Company ID** from the VECTRA participant list. Either **self-register** at [https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com/register](https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com/register) or open the **invitation email** sent to you. Both paths include an activation link (valid 7 days) to set your password. After signing in at [https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com](https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com), open **My courses** to **start, continue, or review** your training. Your progress is saved automatically.
